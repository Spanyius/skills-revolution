About Skills (R)Evolution

European conference series on workforce skills and industrial transformation

The conference brings together public authorities, industry and engineering organisations, social partners, education and training providers, regions, researchers and European projects to discuss skills requirements and implementation approaches.

EU
Purpose

Linking skills policy, industrial needs and implementation

Initiated by the Automotive Skills Alliance, Skills (R)Evolution focuses on workforce development in the automotive-mobility ecosystem and related strategic sectors. It provides a structured setting for comparing evidence, policy priorities and implementation experience.

The programme addresses competence requirements associated with electrification, digitalisation, automation, sustainability, circularity, changing value chains and new industrial business models.

Core topics

Recurring areas of discussion

Skills intelligence

Identification and interpretation of changing competence, occupation and workforce requirements.

Education and training

Curriculum development, training delivery, modular learning and industry-provider cooperation.

Recognition

Competence recognition, quality assurance, micro-credentials and lifelong-learning mechanisms.

Regional implementation

Translation of European and sectoral priorities into regional workforce-development activities.

Industrial transition

Skills implications of technology adoption, decarbonisation, digitalisation and changing value chains.

European coordination

Interaction between public authorities, Pact for Skills partnerships, industry, projects and education systems.

Participants

Stakeholders represented in the conference

Participants include European, national and regional policymakers; companies and engineering organisations; sector associations and clusters; universities and VET providers; social partners; researchers; and teams working in European skills and industrial-transition projects.

The conference is intended as a working forum for presenting evidence, comparing approaches, discussing implementation barriers and identifying areas for cooperation.

European context

Sectoral cooperation under the European skills agenda

Skills (R)Evolution is connected to the Pact for Skills and to European cooperation on workforce development. Previous editions have also been linked with sectoral Blueprint projects and other initiatives supporting implementation.
