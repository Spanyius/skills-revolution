The conference series

Skills, transformation and industrial collaboration

Skills (R)Evolution is a European conference series dedicated to the future of skills in the automotive-mobility ecosystem and related strategic industries. It connects strategic policy debate with practical approaches to education, training, upskilling, reskilling and skills intelligence.
