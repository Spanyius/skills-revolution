About the conference

The annual flagship event of the Automotive Skills Alliance

Skills (R)Evolution is the yearly flagship event of the Automotive Skills Alliance (ASA), the Pact for Skills Large-Scale Partnership for the automotive-mobility ecosystem. It gathers partners from industry, education and training, regions, social partners and EU institutions to exchange experience, align priorities and strengthen cooperation in implementation of Europe’s skills agenda.

The 2027 Stuttgart edition will focus on coordinated action for competitiveness, innovation and a just industrial transition, connecting European strategy with concrete regional, sectoral and project implementation.

Audience

Who the conference is for

ASA partners and members, European and national authorities, regional actors, industry and cluster representatives, education and training providers, social partners and related European initiatives.

Purpose

What the conference aims to achieve

Take stock of achievements, identify shared priorities and plan the next steps for a coordinated European skills response that supports competitiveness, innovation and a just transition.
