The Skills (R)evolution Conference brings together industry leaders, education and training providers, policymakers, regions, social partners and skills experts from across Europe to shape the future of skills in the automotive and mobility ecosystem, connected industrial ecosystem and beyond. It connects strategic priorities with real industry needs and practical education and training solutions, creating a unique space to exchange insights, showcase good practices, strengthen collaboration and jointly address the skills needed for Europe’s competitiveness, innovation and ongoing technological transformation.

Focus areas

Turning Europe’s skills priorities into action across industries and regions

The conference translates European skills ambitions into practical cooperation for the automotive-mobility ecosystem. It connects the Pact for Skills, the Union of Skills and the Industrial Action Plan for the European automotive sector with regional delivery, cross-sector partnerships and proven approaches from industry and education.

01

European skills priorities

Strategic input on the Union of Skills, sectoral skills agendas, implementation and the role of European and regional partnerships.

02

Regional & cross-sector collaboration

Cooperation that supports industrial transformation across automotive-mobility, technology ecosystems and regions.

03

Good practices

Upskilling and reskilling examples from Blueprint projects, TRIREME, national and regional initiatives and industrial partners.

04

Education & industry

Partnerships that strengthen STEM, attract talent and improve workforce readiness for technological change.

About the conference

The annual flagship event of the Automotive Skills Alliance

Conference organiser

The Skills (R)evolution Conference brings together industry, education, policymakers and skills experts to shape the future of skills in automotive, mobility and connected industries. It creates a space for collaboration, exchange and joint action on Europe’s competitiveness and transformation.

The 2027 Stuttgart edition will focus on coordinated action for competitiveness, innovation and a just industrial transition, connecting European strategy with concrete regional, sectoral and project implementation.

Audience

Who the conference is for

ASA partners and members, European and national authorities, regional actors, industry and cluster representatives, education and training providers, social partners and related European initiatives.

Purpose

What the conference aims to achieve

Take stock of achievements, identify shared priorities and plan the next steps for a coordinated European skills response that supports competitiveness, innovation and a just transition.
