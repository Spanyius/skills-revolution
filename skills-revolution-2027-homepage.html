Conference focus

From skills intelligence to implementation

The programme will address how European and sectoral skills priorities are translated into workforce planning, competence development, education and training, recognition, regional implementation and cooperation between industrial ecosystems.

01

Skills intelligence

Evidence on changing occupations, competence requirements, technology trends and workforce demand.

02

Industrial transition

Implications of electrification, digitalisation, automation, sustainability and cross-sector convergence for skills.

03

Education and training

Methods for updating curricula, training offers, qualifications and competence-recognition approaches.

04

European implementation

Coordination between policy, industry, regions, social partners and EU-funded initiatives.

Conference series

A forum for sectoral skills policy and implementation

Skills (R)Evolution is a European conference series initiated by the Automotive Skills Alliance. It provides a forum for discussing workforce transformation in the automotive-mobility ecosystem and related strategic sectors, with particular attention to skills intelligence, education and training, competence recognition, regional implementation and European cooperation.
