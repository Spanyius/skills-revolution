This conference has concluded. Registration has been removed and the programme is preserved as a historical archive.

Conference focus

From skills intelligence to training delivery.

The 2024 edition connected policymakers, industry and education stakeholders around practical challenges in gathering and updating skills intelligence, translating it into education and training, and recognising competencies.

The event also marked the public kick-off of TRIREME, a four-year Erasmus+ Blueprint initiative supporting the transformation of skills across the automotive-mobility ecosystem.

The public launch of TRIREME

The conference was organised in the framework of the TRIREME Erasmus+ Blueprint project, with a focus on skills intelligence, job-role needs, education and training development, recognition, digitalisation, sustainability, circular economy, value-chain resilience and the social dimension of the twin transition.
