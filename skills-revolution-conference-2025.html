Conference focus

From European strategy to hands-on regional examples.

The Ostrava edition combined high-level discussion on the future of the European automotive-mobility skills agenda with practical examples from industry, education, regional initiatives and European projects.

The programme connected the Union of Skills and Pact for Skills context with TRIREME results, Moravian-Silesian good practices, green transition initiatives, digitalisation and collaboration across the ecosystem.

Project context

TRIREME, FLAMENCO and GREEN synergies

The conference was organised in the framework of the TRIREME Erasmus+ Blueprint project and highlighted ongoing work on skills intelligence, training development, competence recognition and regional rollout.

The 2025 edition also benefited from synergies with the FLAMENCO project and the European Greener VET Network (GREEN), extending the focus on collaboration, skills development and sustainability under the Pact for Skills.
