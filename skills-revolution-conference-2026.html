Conference focus

Cross-sector skills strategies for Europe’s industrial transformation.

The 2026 edition brought the automotive-mobility and semiconductor ecosystems together around the Union of Skills, Pact for Skills actions, workforce transformation, sectoral skills intelligence, education and training, and regional implementation.

Hosted in Cluj-Napoca, the event combined policy dialogue and industrial perspectives with Blueprint project results, Romanian good practices, university showcases and networking.

European context

Automotive and semiconductor ecosystems in one skills conversation.

The 2026 edition was organised in the framework of TRIREME and in collaboration with the European Chips Skills Academy. It reflected the growing convergence between automotive-mobility and semiconductor value chains and the need for coordinated European skills strategies.

The conference supported Pact for Skills collaboration by bringing together large-scale partnerships, projects, industrial stakeholders, regions and education providers.
