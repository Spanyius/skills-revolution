About Skills (R)Evolution

A European conference series on skills, transformation and industrial collaboration.

Skills (R)Evolution brings together policymakers, industry, social partners, education and training providers, regions, researchers and European initiatives to shape a future-ready workforce.

EU
Our mission

Connecting policy with practical implementation.

Launched by the Automotive Skills Alliance (ASA), Skills (R)Evolution started as the annual conference of the alliance and has developed into a broader European platform for cooperation on education, training, reskilling, upskilling and skills intelligence.

The series responds to a central European challenge: how to equip people, organisations and regions with the right competences for electrification, digitalisation, automation, sustainability, circular economy, resilient value chains and changing business models.

How the series evolved

From Stuttgart to a cross-sector European platform

Each edition builds on the same mission while reflecting the most relevant skills challenges of its time.

  1. 2022 · Stuttgart

    The first face-to-face ASA conference focused on upskilling, reskilling, regional cooperation and skills intelligence.

  2. 2024 · Brussels

    The conference hosted the public kick-off of the TRIREME Blueprint project and linked conference dialogue with long-term European project implementation.

  3. 2025 · Ostrava

    A stronger practice-oriented edition highlighted regional examples, education innovation, collaborative initiatives and project synergies.

  4. 2026 · Cluj-Napoca

    The series broadened across strategic sectors, connecting automotive-mobility with semiconductors and coordinated European skills strategies.

  5. 2027 · Stuttgart

    The conference returns to Stuttgart in April 2027. Exact date, venue and programme will be announced.

European context

A bridge between strategy, projects, regions and action.

Skills (R)Evolution is closely connected to the Pact for Skills and European cooperation on workforce development. Recent editions have created strong links with major initiatives including TRIREME and other sectoral projects.

The distinctive value of the series is its combination of strategy, collaboration and practical relevance: participants exchange evidence, compare approaches, align priorities and build partnerships rather than only discussing trends.
